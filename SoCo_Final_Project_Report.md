## 7. Limitations
- The sampled area is based on the project's existing geographic coverage; it is not necessarily an official South Congress Avenue boundary.
- OpenStreetMap business names, categories, and road names may be missing, outdated, or inconsistently tagged.
- A nearest-road match is a geometric proximity result and does not prove that a business has a South Congress Avenue address.
- Cluster IDs are algorithm-generated labels and do not represent quality or importance by themselves.
- The ranking formula uses chosen weights. Results may change if the weights, clustering parameters, or geographic study area change.

## 8. Recommendations
1. Define the study corridor using a verified geographic boundary or an explicitly documented street segment.
2. Filter businesses by their actual proximity to the selected road or corridor polygon, rather than relying only on cluster membership.
3. Review missing business names and generic categories to improve data quality.
4. Compare business density and category diversity within the validated corridor.
5. Keep the scoring formula and its weights visible in presentations so the ranking is transparent.
